Getting started

In about five minutes you will install Stockyard, open a live Bitcoin chart, add an indicator, and save a watchlist.

Install Stockyard

  1. Open the latest release.
  2. Download the installer for your computer:
    • Mac (Apple Silicon or Intel): the .dmg file.
    • Windows: the -setup.exe file.
  3. Install the app:
    • Mac: open the .dmg and drag Stockyard into Applications.
    • Windows: run the .exe.

The installers are not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer account yet, so your computer asks once before the first launch:

  • Mac: open Stockyard and click Done on the warning. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  • Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

Stockyard updates itself after that. When a new version is out, a banner offers Update & restart.

Take the tour

On first launch, a short tour points at each part of the app. Click Next or press the right arrow key to move through it. Press Esc to leave it at any time.

To see the tour again, open Settings → About and click Take the tour again.

Open a chart

  1. Type ETH anywhere in the window. A search box opens with your letters in it.
  2. Choose ETH-USD. The chart switches to Ethereum and streams live trades.
  3. Click 1h in the timeframe bar. You can also type a timeframe such as 2h or 3D into the timeframe menu.

Add an indicator

  1. Click ƒx Indicators.
  2. Search for RSI and click it. A new panel with the RSI line appears under the chart.
  3. Point at the RSI name in the chart legend and click the gear to change its length, colors, or line style.

To save the indicators you have on the chart as one set, open ƒx Indicators → Stacks and click Save stack. Apply the stack to any chart later with one click.

Build a watchlist

  1. Open the watchlist on the right. If it is hidden, click Watchlist in the top-right corner.
  2. Click Add symbol (the + at the top of the panel) and add a few symbols.
  3. Drag a row to reorder it. Click a row to open that symbol on the chart.

To see the same list as colored tiles, switch the watchlist view to Heatmap.

Add US stocks (optional)

Crypto works with no account. For US stocks, ETFs, and options, connect a free Alpaca account or your Tradier account. Alpaca is the quickest to set up:

  1. Make a free account at alpaca.markets. You don't need to deposit money.
  2. On your paper trading dashboard, open API Keys and generate a key.
  3. In Stockyard, open Settings → Data sources, paste the key ID and the secret into the Alpaca card, and click Save & test.

To use Tradier instead, paste your Tradier token into the Tradier card. A free developer sandbox gives delayed data, and a brokerage account gives real-time data. If you connect both, US stocks data source picks which one Stockyard uses.

Stockyard tests your keys, then saves them in your computer's keychain. Stocks such as AAPL and SPY now appear in search next to crypto, and the options chain button in the top bar shows their options.
