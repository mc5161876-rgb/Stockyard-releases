The Stockyard assistant

The assistant is a chat beside your charts. Ask in plain words, and it works Stockyard for you. It opens charts, changes timeframes, adds indicators, draws, sets alerts and changes layouts.

  • "Mark support and resistance on BTC."
  • "Add a 200 EMA and alert me if the price crosses it."
  • "Show ETH and SOL side by side on the daily chart."

The assistant uses the same commands as outside AI agents, so it can do everything they can. It only reads market data. It can't place trades or move money.

The assistant works in the desktop app. You bring the AI yourself. Use your own Anthropic or OpenAI API key and pay them for what you use, or run a free model on your own computer with Ollama. Stockyard never signs in to a Claude or ChatGPT subscription.

Open the assistant

Click the chat bubble in the top bar, press ⌘J (Ctrl+J on Windows), or choose Show or hide the assistant in the command bar. Press ⌘J again to hide it. Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider.

Set it up

Open Settings → Assistant, or click Set up the assistant in the panel.

With Claude or GPT

  1. Under Who answers, pick Claude (Anthropic) or GPT (OpenAI).
  2. Make an API key at console.anthropic.com or platform.openai.com.
  3. Paste it into the key box and press Save & test. Stockyard checks the key with the provider before it saves it.
  4. Pick a model. Claude Sonnet 5.5 is the default. To use a model that isn't listed, choose Another model and type its id.

With Ollama, for free

  1. Install Ollama and download a model that can use tools, such as qwen3 or llama3.1.
  2. Under Who answers, pick Ollama (free).
  3. Leave the address at http://localhost:11434, or type the address of a computer on your home network. Press Test connection.
  4. Pick a model from the list. Can act marks models that can use Stockyard's tools. Sees charts marks models that can look at chart pictures.

Local models are slower and make more mistakes than Claude or GPT. They also get a shorter list of 21 core commands, so they fit in a small context window.

Choose what the assistant may do

Settings → Assistant → What the assistant may do works like the level for agents, but it is separate. You can let the assistant act while outside agents stay off.

Level What the assistant can do
Off Nothing. Stockyard sends no requests.
Read only Look at charts, prices, drawings, alerts and settings. Each change is refused, and the assistant tells you so.
Ask before acting (default) Look freely. Each change shows a prompt. Press Allow or Deny.
Full control Change anything without asking.

Only you can change this level. The assistant can't read or change it, and neither can agents. It stays on this computer and doesn't sync.

Every call the assistant makes shows in Settings → Agents → What agents did, under the name "Assistant".

Use the chat

  • Each step shows as a line under the reply. The line says what the step did and whether it was done, refused, failed or not run. A refused or failed line also says why.
  • Stop ends the request. A change that is waiting for your answer is denied.
  • The assistant stops after 15 steps for one message, so a request can't run up your bill. Press Keep going to let it continue.
  • New chat (the plus button) clears the conversation.
  • The chat is saved on this computer, so it is still there after a restart. Chart pictures are not saved.

When something goes wrong, the panel says what happened. A rejected key or a missing model has an Open settings button. A busy provider, a rate limit or a lost connection has Try again. If Ollama isn't running, the panel says so. Open the Ollama app and try again.

What leaves your computer

With Claude or GPT, each message sends these to Anthropic or OpenAI:

  • what you type, and the chat so far;
  • the current time, your time zone and the active chart's symbol and timeframe;
  • whatever the assistant reads in Stockyard, such as prices, your drawings and alerts, and chart pictures.

With Ollama, all of this stays on your computer or your home network.

Your API keys stay in your computer's keychain, which is Keychain on Mac and Credential Manager on Windows. Only the app's native backend reads them. The chat, plugins, agents and sync never see them.

How it works

This section is for contributors.

  • Keys and network. src-tauri/src/assistant.rs keeps the keys and makes every request. It only calls Anthropic's messages and models endpoints, OpenAI's chat completions and models endpoints, and the Ollama address you set. That address must be this computer or one on a private network. It never follows redirects.
  • One conversation, three adapters. src/assistant/conversation.ts holds the chat in one shape. src/assistant/providers.ts turns it into each provider's request and reads each answer back. Anthropic's own reply blocks are kept and sent back unchanged.
  • The loop. src/assistant/loop.ts sends the chat, runs the tool calls one at a time, and sends the results back. It stops at 15 steps or when you press Stop. Each tool call goes through callCommand with the client name "Assistant", so access levels, the approval prompt and the action log apply.
  • Tests. tests/assistant*.test.ts cover the adapters, the loop and the access rules. e2e/assistant.spec.ts runs the panel against a scripted fake model in the fake desktop.
