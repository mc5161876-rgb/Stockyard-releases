Privacy, safety, and questions

Does Stockyard cost anything?

No. Stockyard is free and open source under the Apache 2.0 license. Crypto data from Coinbase is free. Tradier offers a free developer sandbox with delayed stock data.

Can Stockyard trade or move my money?

No. Stockyard reads market data only. It has no code path to orders or to your brokerage account. The Tradier connection can reach only market-data endpoints: quotes, history, time and sales, symbol lookup, and the market clock and calendar.

Where is my Tradier token stored?

In your computer's keychain: Keychain on Mac and Credential Manager on Windows. Stockyard tests the token with Tradier before it saves it. After that, only the app's native backend reads it. The chart window, plugins, custom indicators, and AI agents never see it.

Does Stockyard send my data anywhere?

Stockyard talks to Coinbase and Tradier to get market data, and checks the public Stockyard releases page for updates. Widgets you add fetch their own data: live scores from ESPN, the X feed from X with your key, and Fear & Greed from alternative.me. Stock logos in the watchlist load from financialmodelingprep.com. Stockyard has no account system, no analytics, and no tracking.

Are custom indicators and plugins safe?

Custom indicators run in a sandbox with no access to your keys or the internet. Plugins are full JavaScript files, so install only plugins from people you trust, the same as any app add-on.

Why does my computer warn me the first time I open Stockyard?

The installers are not signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer account. Getting started shows the one extra click you need on first launch. After that, updates are signed with the Stockyard project's own key, and the app checks that signature before it installs an update.

Which computers does Stockyard run on?

Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows 10 or 11.

Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on the Stockyard releases repository.
