Feature guide

This page lists what each part of Stockyard does and where to find it. Almost every choice on this page is a setting. Change it from Customize in the top bar, from Settings (the gear, or ⌘, on Mac), or from the panel itself.

Charts

  • Symbols. Start typing anywhere, or press /, to search crypto and stocks together.
  • Timeframes. Pick a favorite in the top bar, choose from the menu, or type your own, such as 2h, 45, or 3D. Weeks, months, and years work too.
  • Chart types. Candles, hollow candles, Heikin Ashi, bars, line, area, and baseline.
  • Chart settings. Candle colors, status line, log and percent scales, a countdown to bar close, background, grid, crosshair, and watermark. Open them from Settings → Chart or by right-clicking the chart.
  • Reset the view. Right-click the chart and choose Reset chart view, or press Alt+R.
  • History. Scroll left to load older bars.

Indicators

  • Click ƒx Indicators to open the shop. It has 60 built-in indicators with TradingView's default settings, such as moving averages, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, Ichimoku, SuperTrend, MACD, RSI, and Stochastic.
  • Every indicator shows a live preview on your current chart before you add it.
  • Point at an indicator in the chart legend to hide it, remove it, or open its settings.
  • Stacks save the indicators on your chart as a named set. Five starter stacks are included.
  • Your own indicators. Write one, paste code, or import a .ts or .js file. Read writing your own indicator.

Drawing tools

  • The rail on the left holds more than 50 tools: lines, channels, pitchforks, Fibonacci and Gann tools, chart patterns, Elliott waves, ranges, long and short positions, shapes, brushes, arrows, and text.
  • Snap to candles is on by default. Points snap to a candle's open, high, low, or close. Hold ⌘ or Ctrl to turn snapping off while you draw.
  • Hold Shift to keep a line horizontal, vertical, or at 45°.
  • Click a drawing to select it. Drag it to move it, or drag its handles to reshape it. The style bar sets color, thickness, line style, lock, copy, and delete.
  • Drawings are saved per symbol and show on every timeframe.
  • Undo with ⌘Z, redo with ⇧⌘Z, and delete with the Delete key.
  • Customize toolbar, at the bottom of the rail, lets you star favorite tools, reorder them, and hide groups.

Layouts and workspaces

  • Click the layout button in the top bar to show one to eight charts at once.
  • Drag, resize, and lock each chart box when you use a free layout.
  • Charts can share their symbol, timeframe, or crosshair.
  • Workspaces save the whole screen: layout, symbols, and indicators. Switch between them from the workspace menu.

Alerts

  • Click Alert, or press Alt+A, to create a price alert. You can also right-click the chart, or select a drawing, to alert on it.
  • Alerts fire a desktop notification and a sound, even when Stockyard is in the background.
  • Click the bell to open the alert manager. Pause, resume, edit, or delete alerts there, and see the log of fired alerts.
  • Drag an alert line on the chart to change its price.

Screener

  • Click Screener in the top bar to filter the whole market by price, volume, and indicators.
  • Click a result to open it on the chart.

Options chain

  • Open a US stock and click the options chain button in the top bar. The chain shows that stock's options, or type another ticker into the panel.
  • Pick an expiration from the row of dates. W marks a weekly and M a monthly.
  • Calls sit on the left, puts on the right, and the strike nearest the stock's price is highlighted. A line marks where the price is.
  • Click a contract to chart it, such as AAPL Dec 18 '26 230 C. Point at a contract and click + to add it to your watchlist.
  • The ⋯ menu picks the columns (bid, ask, volume, open interest, IV, and greeks), how many strikes to show, and whether to show calls and puts side by side or one at a time.
  • The chain refreshes every 15 seconds. It needs a Tradier or Alpaca account, the same as US stocks.

Bar replay

  • Click Replay and pick a starting bar. The chart hides everything after that bar.
  • Press play to bring candles back one at a time at the speed you choose. Use replay to practice reading a chart without knowing what comes next.

Watchlist

  • The watchlist shows logo, symbol, last price, change, and change % in sections you can collapse.
  • Switch between the list view and the heatmap view.
  • Drag rows to reorder them. Double-click a section to rename it.

Themes

  • Stockyard has 13 themes, including light ones. Six have a moving background: Aurora, Synthwave, Digital rain, Deep space, Ember, and Ocean.
  • Make your own in Settings → Themes. Set every color, the moving background, its strength, and its speed.
  • Share a theme as a short code. Paste a code to import someone else's theme.
  • Animation follows your computer's reduce-motion setting. You can also set it to Full, Calm, or Off.

Widgets

  • Open the widgets menu in the top bar to add boxes that float over the chart.
  • Built-in widgets: live sports scores, an X feed (with your own X API key), prices, mini charts, a market clock, Crypto Fear & Greed, notes, and a position size calculator.
  • Drag a widget to move it, drag an edge to resize it, and collapse or lock it from its title bar.
  • Plugins add more widgets and themes. Read plugins.

AI agents

An AI agent can run Stockyard for you: set up charts, add indicators, draw, create alerts, change themes, and more. You choose how much it may do. Read what an agent can do.

The assistant does the same from a chat beside your charts, with your own Anthropic or OpenAI key or a free local model through Ollama.

Data sources

  • Crypto comes from Coinbase. It works with no account.
  • US stocks, ETFs, and options come from your own Tradier account or a free Alpaca account. Set them up in Settings → Data sources, in the desktop app.
    • Alpaca is free with a paper trading account, with no deposit. Live prices are trades on the IEX exchange, history covers the whole market up to 15 minutes ago, and options quotes are delayed.
    • Tradier gives real-time data with a brokerage account, or delayed data with a free developer sandbox.
    • US stocks data source picks which one serves stocks: Auto uses Tradier when its token is saved and Alpaca otherwise, or choose Tradier or Alpaca yourself. Charts and watchlists switch over right away.
  • The badges in the top-right corner show each source's status: Live, Reconnecting, Rate limited, or Set up. Click a badge to open its settings.

Updates

Stockyard checks for a new version each time it starts. When one is ready, click Update & restart. Updates are signed, so the app installs only builds that come from the Stockyard project.
